I am a Ph.D. student in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at the University of Colorado Boulder, working with Dr. Scott Taylor. I am interested in the evolutionary processes that generate and maintain biodiversity, particularly speciation and hybridization in birds.

My research combines population genomics, transcriptomics, and museum collections to investigate genomic divergence, reproductive isolation, and evolutionary change.

Prior to CU Boulder, I received my B.A. in Biology from Pomona College in 2024.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the genomic consequences of hybridization and the evolution of reproductive isolation, particularly among North American chickadees.

I also use historical museum specimens and contemporary genomic data to investigate evolutionary change through time.

## Museum Work

I am interested in the role of natural history collections in evolutionary research, particularly the application of genomic approaches to historical specimens.
